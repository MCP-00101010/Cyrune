# Platform artwork

DOS, Windows, Amiga, FM Towns, Atari ST, Macintosh and ZX Spectrum PNG badges are copied unchanged from [ScummVM's platform icon collection](https://github.com/scummvm/scummvm-icons/tree/fcf8c4ca51f9406e858e301ab66b511b5d3bed0b/default/icons/platforms), revision `fcf8c4ca51f9406e858e301ab66b511b5d3bed0b`. Their corresponding editable SVG sources are included in `sources/`. Original filenames `pc`, `fmtowns`, `atari`, and `zx` are renamed locally to `dos`, `fm-towns`, `atari-st`, and `zx-spectrum`. See `SCUMMVM-LICENSE.txt` (GPL-2.0) and `SCUMMVM-AUTHORS.txt`. These are ScummVM-distributed badges, not a claim of vendor endorsement.

`steam.svg` is copied unchanged from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/777807a262bb7384ff406fd4b35fdcd02e9514c3/icons/steam.svg), revision `777807a262bb7384ff406fd4b35fdcd02e9514c3`. See `STEAM-LICENSE.txt` (CC0). CSS adjusts its display colour. Platform/product trademarks belong to their respective owners.

`unknown.svg` is original Cyrune vector artwork: a green question mark with orange edging inspired by ScummVM's colour palette. It is not copied from ScummVM artwork.

Assets added on 2026-09-07. All images load locally; Arcade does not contact their upstream repositories at runtime.
